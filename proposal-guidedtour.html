About the service

What is a consultation.

This is a consultancy model — not a full campaign. Instead of managing every action of your campaign, Sakae PR guides your team week by week so it can make informed decisions throughout the awards season.

It is ideal for teams that already have their own operational structure but need strategic guidance from someone who knows the rules, rhythms, and nuances of an Oscar campaign in depth. This proposal covers consultancy services for Visite Guidée (Guided Tour), directed and produced by Alba Jaramillo for Premier Cri.

What's included

What's included.

01
WhatsApp group for consultations
Direct access via a dedicated WhatsApp group for questions, material review, and quick guidance throughout the week. An agile communication channel for the day-to-day decisions of the campaign.
02
2-hour weekly consultations
Weekly two-hour sessions, running until the close of shortlist nominations voting — scheduled for December 11. Each session is structured according to the campaign phase and the week's priorities.
03
Orientation about outreach
Guidance on how to approach Academy members according to the Rules and Regulations, press outlets, critics, and other agents relevant to the campaign. Includes support in outreach language, review and approval.
04
Connecting with industry people
We help connect your team with publicists, translators, and moderators who are at the forefront of each category — the right people for the right moment in the campaign.
05
Add-Ons to the Package
We have a team available to design AMPAS e-blasts with Sakae PR's guidance for what works best ($150 per e-blast), FYC cards for social media ($50 per artwork), and social media video editing ($150 per video).
