Reference Archive of Awards Campaigns Graphic Materials

This page displays examples of graphic materials produced for past Academy Awards campaigns: invitations, screening notices, trade ads and other For Your Consideration assets. It is a working reference for designers, publicists and producers. Use it to check what has already been approved, how other campaigns solved layout and hierarchy problems, and what the Academy actually accepts — which is often narrower than what looks good.

Category 01

Examples of AMPAS-Approved Invites
to Be Sent via Hazmat

Official digital graphic piece designed specifically to invite Academy voters to screenings or notify them about the film. All mailings to Academy members go through the Academy-approved mailing house (Hazmat), and every piece is reviewed against the Academy's Campaign Promotional Regulations before it goes out.

✕   What is NOT ALLOWED
  • Review quotes
  • Laurels or awards
  • Unrelated logos
  • Oscar statuette, Academy logos, or Oscars logos
  • Other links or references to other films
  • Images, photos, key art, or QR codes — except the title treatment (film title artwork)
✓   What is ALLOWED
  • For Your Consideration + Category the film is competing
  • Clear and unembellished synopsis, up to 300 characters including spaces
  • Screening information, in one of two forms:
    • In-person theatrical screenings, with date, time and location
    • For films on the Academy Screening Room (ASR): "WATCH NOW" plus the ASR link
  • Event details for Q&As, panel discussions or receptions
  • Contact email / RSVP (usually rsvp@sakaepr.com)
  • Studio or distributor logos directly associated with the film
